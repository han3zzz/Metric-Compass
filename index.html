How the model works

Each bin is a price level: bins above the price hold token 0 and bins below hold token 1, and a trade crossing a bin converts it at that bin's price. Trading PnL is the position value minus holding the initial tokens, as in Edge Lab. Distributions: Flat is equal weight; Exponential fades from the price outward over a third of the range; Inverted puts more at the edges; Volume Pro-Rata follows where trades happen; APR Pro-Rata follows volume divided by the liquidity already there (flatter than volume); Single bin puts everything at the price. Fees: trading volume is spread over price levels with a Laplace shape (reach λ) that follows the price along a straight path to the scenario price, the pool's existing liquidity is assumed more spread out than volume, and you earn your share of each bin's fees, times the LP part of the spread (100% minus curator share minus Metric's share). Stop-loss: Metric's exact drawdown rule is not in the public data here, so the simulator treats the limit as Trading PnL loss as a percent of capital, checked after each step of the jump. Simplifications: the deposit goes straight into the chosen distribution with no cost for swapping into that mix, volume does not react to spread changes, and the price path is a line. Seven days of data can flip quickly. Not Metric's model and not financial advice.
